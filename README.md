## Overview

**Legally Blonde** is a classic 2001 American comedy film directed by *Robert Luketic* and based on Amanda Brown's 2001 novel of the same name. It tells the story of **Elle Woods**, a sorority girl who attempts to win back her ex-boyfriend Warner Huntington III by getting a Juris Doctor degree at Harvard Law School, and in the process, overcomes stereotypes against blondes and triumphs as a successful lawyer.
